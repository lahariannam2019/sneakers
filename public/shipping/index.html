Find information about shipping times, delivery regions, tracking, and shipping costs for all Sneakers orders.

Shipping & Delivery

At Sneako, we make sure every order is packed carefully and delivered as quickly as possible. From order processing to final delivery, we’re committed to providing a smooth and reliable shopping experience for every customer worldwide.

Processing Time

All orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days after payment confirmation.

Orders placed during weekends, holidays, or limited product drops may require additional processing time due to high order volume. Once your order has been shipped, you’ll receive a confirmation email with tracking details.

Please note that processing times are separate from shipping times.

Shipping Times

Estimated delivery times may vary depending on your location and local courier services.


Delivery estimates are provided as guidelines and may occasionally be affected by customs processing, weather conditions, or carrier delays.

Shipping Costs

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your delivery location and preferred shipping method.

We offer free standard shipping on qualifying orders. Express shipping options may be available in selected regions.

Please note that customs duties, taxes, and import fees are not included in the shipping cost and may apply for international orders depending on local regulations.

Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, you’ll receive an email containing your tracking number and shipment details.

You can use the tracking link provided in your confirmation email to monitor your package in real time throughout the delivery process.

If you haven’t received tracking information within 3 business days of placing your order, please contact our support team.

International Shipping

Sneako ships to selected countries worldwide.

International delivery times may vary depending on destination, customs clearance procedures, and local shipping carriers. Customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes that may apply upon arrival.

We recommend checking your country’s import regulations before placing an international order.

Packaging

Every pair of sneakers is packaged with care to ensure maximum protection during transit.

Our packaging is designed to keep your order secure while maintaining the premium unboxing experience Sneako is known for.

Lost or Delayed Packages

While we work closely with trusted shipping partners, occasional delays may occur during transit.

If your package appears delayed, lost, or marked as delivered incorrectly, please contact our support team and we’ll assist you as quickly as possible.

Sneako is not responsible for delays caused by customs clearance, weather disruptions, or incorrect shipping information provided at checkout.

Signature Delivery

For security purposes, selected orders may require a signature upon delivery.

This helps ensure your sneakers arrive safely and are received by the correct recipient.

Returns & Exchanges

Need to return or exchange your order?

Visit our Returns & Exchanges page for detailed information regarding eligibility, return windows, and exchange policies.
